Thoughts

Life

Starting over in a new country

Moving abroad means rebuilding your routines, your network and sometimes your confidence. It also shows you what you're capable of.

Learning

Start at the bottom and learn the whole picture

Starting on the shop floor taught me what the work looks like from the other side of the drawing. It's a habit I'd recommend in any field.

Technology

Automate the boring task you do every week

If a task is frequent, rule-based and error-prone, a small tool usually pays back fast. A 30-minute routine of mine became a 60-second one.

AI

AI is a tool, and you still sign off

AI speeds up drafting and formatting, but the person is still responsible for checking the facts.
